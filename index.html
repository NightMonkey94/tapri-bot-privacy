Tapri Bot privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Tapri Bot ("the game") is made by Suraj Manickam. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: surajimmanuel@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress and stars, coins, your stall's decor and the robot's outfits, the programs you build for each level, your settings, your Daily Challenge results and streak, and your Rush Hour best are saved only on your phone. We never see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game sends, and why

The game uses three Google services. Each one receives a little data from your phone:

  • Ads (Google AdMob). To show ads and count them, AdMob receives your phone's advertising ID, an approximate location worked out from your internet address, basic device information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings, Privacy choices.
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). To learn which levels are too hard and where players get stuck, the game sends events about play: levels started, finished or left, stars, blocks and steps used, time taken, the kind of mistake in a failed run, hints used, Daily Challenge and Rush Hour results, ads watched, decor bought and purchases made. These come with an app instance ID and basic device information. They never include your name, contacts, messages, the programs you build, or anything you type.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics). When the game crashes or an error happens, it sends a report of where in the game it happened, with basic device information and an installation ID, so we can fix it.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought, never your card or bank details.

Sharing your daily result

Your Daily Challenge result is shared only when you tap Share, and only through the app you choose. The text says the daily's number, your stars, blocks and steps, and nothing else.

Who receives the data

Data sent to AdMob and Firebase is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and their terms for these services. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

How long it's kept

Analytics event data is kept for 2 months, data tied to your app instance ID for up to 14 months, and crash reports for about 90 days, under Google's settings for these services.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent under Settings, Privacy choices, where it applies to you.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings: Settings, Google, Ads.
  • Ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance ID by writing to surajimmanuel@gmail.com.
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything saved on your phone.

Children

Tapri Bot is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data was sent about them, write to surajimmanuel@gmail.com and we will ask Google to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and at https://nightmonkey94.github.io/tapri-bot-privacy/, with a new date at the top.
